Project 2: Word Counter

Build a frequency distribution map. This structure is foundational for processing text-based inputs and log parsing pipelines.

  • Parse complete sentences into dynamic lists with .split().
  • Safely check for existing map keys with the in operator.
  • Dynamically initialize counters or increment tracked frequencies.
Common Pitfall Attempting to modify or increment a key that does not exist in a dictionary yet will trigger a immediate KeyError. Always guard your mutations using membership assertions (if word in count) or defaults!
